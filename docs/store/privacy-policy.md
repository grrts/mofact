# Mofact privacy policy

_Last updated: 7 November 2026_

Mofact is a single-player game. **We do not collect, store, sell or share any
personal data.** The game has no account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and
no chat, and it plays fully offline.

## What stays on your device

Your saved games and settings (sound, colour-blind mode and the like) are kept
on your device only. We never receive them.

## Buying the full game

The full game is one purchase, made through Google Play, the App Store or Steam.
That store handles the payment under its own privacy policy, and we never see
your payment details.

On Android and iOS, the game checks that the purchase is yours through
RevenueCat, a service that verifies in-app purchases. For that, the store's
receipt for the purchase and a random identifier made up by the app (not your
name, email or device id) are sent to RevenueCat, with the technical details
any such request carries (app and system version, language and country). They
are used only to unlock
the game on your devices and restore the purchase, never for advertising.
RevenueCat's own policy is at https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy.

## Cloud saves

If cloud saves are turned on in a later version, they will use your platform's
own cloud: Google Play Games on Android, iCloud on iOS and Steam Cloud on PC.
Your saves then live in your own account with that platform, under its privacy
policy, and we cannot read them.

## Children

Mofact is suitable for all ages and collects nothing from anyone, children
included.

## Permissions

On Android the game asks for network access (only to check the purchase and,
later, for cloud saves) and for vibration (for haptics). It asks for nothing
else: no location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a
new date. We will never start collecting personal data without asking you first.

## Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue at https://github.com/grrts/mofact/issues
or write to the support address on the game's store page.
