How to play

  • Move: drag with one finger to look around, pinch to zoom. Tap a machine to inspect it.
  • Belt: drag a path. Belts carry items the way you drew them. Two fingers still pan and zoom.
  • Drill goes on ore (blue is iron, orange is copper). Furnace smelts ore into plates. Assembler crafts the recipe you pick.
  • Machines push their output out of the side with the arrow. They take input from any other side.
  • Everything belted into the hub counts towards your goal. Buildings are paid for with plates from the hub.
  • Zoom out to see the factory as a flow map. A red ! marks a machine that is starving for input. A yellow = marks one whose output is backed up.
